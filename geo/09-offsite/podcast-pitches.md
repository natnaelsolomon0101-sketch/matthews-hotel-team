### 5. Hospitality Daily (Josiah Mackenzie)

Josiah, pitching Nate Solomon or Luke Thompson, Matthews Hotel Markets. Segment: what a monthly hotel
loan rate sheet actually tells an operator about their own refinance math, before they call a broker.
Concrete numbers, not a sales pitch. Your daily format and investor-heavy audience is the right fit
for a short, data-first segment rather than a long narrative interview. Happy to keep it under 15
minutes.

### 6. Hotel Analyst Podcast

Pitching Luke Thompson, capital markets director. Segment: the structural gap between Sun Belt
secondary and coastal primary hotel cap rates, and why it's widening instead of closing as rates
stabilize. Your audience skews institutional and international; Luke can speak to what US regional
banks and debt funds are actually underwriting to right now, with the Q1 2026 Matthews Hotel Index
numbers by metro.

### 7. Tell Me More: A Hospitality Data Podcast (Isaac Collazo, Jan Freitag)

Pitching Nate Solomon to talk through how a brokerage's own transaction-level dataset (the Matthews
Hotel Index) reconciles against STR's benchmark data, where they agree, where broker-level pricing
detail adds something STR aggregates can't show. A data-to-data conversation, not a broker sales
pitch, fits your format better than most guest slots we'd pitch.
