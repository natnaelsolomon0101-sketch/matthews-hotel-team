# Guest-post / byline pitches: 10 new outlets

These are new. They do not duplicate the 4 ready-to-send press pitches already at
`content/outreach/press/` (Jeff Weinstein/Hotel Investment Today, CoStar News, LODGING, Bisnow) or
the Tier 1 national list in `content/pr/reporter-targets.md` (WSJ, Bloomberg, Reuters, NYT, Forbes),
which are cold pitches for coverage, not contributed bylines. All 10 below correspond to rows in
`targets.csv`.

Voice: plain, specific, numbers before adjectives, no em-dashes, one ask per pitch. Every number
cited traces to a live, sourced page on the site: the Trepp CMBS figures HUMAN_QUEUE.md already
cleared as "ship as-is" (`hotel-cmbs-distress-trepp-2026.ts`), the Q1 2026 Matthews Hotel Index
(`/research/mhi/q1-2026`), or the already-published cap-rate-compression figures in
`texas-hotel-cap-rates-q2-2026.ts`. None of these pitches uses the disputed $30B CMBS figure, the
unverified Scottsdale RevPAR number, or the unverified AHLA sold-room-nights figure; all three are
still open disputes per `HUMAN_QUEUE.md` and out of bounds until Nate resolves them.
